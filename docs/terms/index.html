Signing in with Google (optional)

Signing in is optional and is not needed to play stations. If you sign in with Google, Radiova uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to keep your favorite stations and a few settings in sync between your browsers. You must be entitled to use the Google account you sign in with, and Google's own terms apply to it. You can delete your account at any time with "Delete account" in the account menu of the site or in the dashboard of the extension; this removes your account and the synced copy. Local data in your browser is separate, and you can clear it on the Privacy page.
