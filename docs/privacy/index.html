Privacy Policy

Radiova respects your privacy. All data is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. No personal information is collected, transmitted, or shared.

What data we store

  • Your favorite stations
  • Listen history
  • App settings (language, theme, volume)
  • Custom playlists
  • Offline app shell and playlist cache

Third-party services

Radiova fetches station data from the open radiova-stations GitHub repository. Stream audio is played directly from source URLs. No third-party analytics or tracking is used.
