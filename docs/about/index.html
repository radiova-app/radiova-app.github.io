About Radiova

Radiova is a cross-platform radio player that lets you listen to stations from around the world.

Playlists

Default playlists are curated for Ukrainian, English, German, and Global stations. All data is fetched from the open radiova-stations repository.

Privacy

All user data is stored locally in your browser.
